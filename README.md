## Overview

**Energy Forecasting Agent** is a reference AI workflow for Energy & Utilities organizations. It addresses a practical finance challenge:

> Energy teams need reliable scenarios across consumption, weather, historical demand and market signals.

The workflow is triggered when **Forecasting cycle or market change**. It coordinates Consumption, weather and scenario agents and keeps people responsible for decisions that require judgment, exception handling, or a sensitive business action.

## Why This AI Workflow Matters

Organizations often have the data and systems needed to solve this problem, but the information is spread across teams and operational tools. An AI agent workflow can help bring the right context together, explain the next step, and carry out approved actions without hiding the decision trail.

This is an architecture pattern, not a promise of automatic results. Production implementation depends on data quality, system access, workflow evaluation, security controls, and a clear human review design.

## How It Works

1. **Combine demand and weather data**
2. **Generate forecast scenarios**
3. **Analyze market signals**
4. **Explain uncertainty**
5. **Prepare recommendations for review**

## AI Agents Involved

Consumption, weather and scenario agents.

## Human Oversight and Security Controls

**Human checkpoint:** Review scenarios and make operational or commercial decisions.

Recommended controls for this AI agent use case:

- Data lineage
- Scenario versioning
- Analyst review
- Approved sources

These controls define what the AI system can see, recommend, or do, and when a person must review the outcome. High-impact actions should fail closed when identity, policy, or approval information is missing.
